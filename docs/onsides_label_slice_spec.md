# OnSIDES later label slice: identifier bridge, report schema and qualification rule

Specification of evidence class E2's later slice (design section 4.2), as implemented in mechanistic_pathway_learning/evidence/onsides_identifier_bridge.py, mechanistic_pathway_learning/evidence/load_onsides_label_events.py, experiments/fetch_onsides_identifier_bridge.py and experiments/build_onsides_reports.py. This page says what the columns and counts mean. Counts here are from the build of 5 October 2026 on OnSIDES v3.1.1 (data release 2026-04-22), ChEMBL 37 and SIDER 4.1, the build released as data/releases/v0.4/onsides; "build of 5 October 2026" below means that build. The generated page docs/generated/onsides_label_slice.md carries the per-symptom, per-country and per-section tables of that build, the last to write it (commit d00ddc5); the builds of scripts/rebuild_data_layer.sh write their pages to data/processed/rebuild_logs/ instead (--markdown-output). The bridge, the builder and the loader have changed since (sections 2, 4, 5 and 7 say where), and docs/symptom_crosswalk.csv gained preferred terms and twelve symptoms on 7 and 9 October 2026, so a rebuild would match more statements than counted here. MedDRA term names are never written to a committed file; identifiers and target symptoms only.

## 1. Inputs

OnSIDES v3.1.1 parquet tables under data/raw/onsides/v3.1.1/parquet: product_adverse_effect (6,928,666 label-event mentions with effect_meddra_id, label_section, match_method and pred1), product_label (41,119 labels with source US, UK, EU or JP), product_to_rxnorm, vocab_rxnorm_ingredient_to_product, vocab_rxnorm_ingredient (1,866 ingredients keyed by RxCUI or an OMOP extension id) and vocab_meddra_adverse_effect (6,423 terms, 3,550 PT and 2,873 LLT). ChEMBL caches under data/raw/chembl: mechanisms.json, targets.json (extended in place by the bridge fetch), molecule_parents.json and pubchem_to_chembl_with_parents.json (SIDER's own ChEMBL mapping). SIDER 4.1 under data/raw/sider_4.1: drug_names.tsv, drug_atc.tsv and meddra_all_se.tsv.gz.

The bridge fetch reads pubchem_to_chembl_with_parents.json as fetched, deliberately not through read_pubchem_to_chembl and configs/chembl_parent_corrections.csv as the SIDER route does since 20 November 2026: that table was derived from the bridge, and tests/test_chembl_parent_corrections.py uses the bridge as the independent witness of each drug's parent. Curated tables read with these: configs/chembl_action_conflict_resolutions.csv, configs/drug_target_exclusions.csv and configs/drug_target_additions.csv, which the mechanism targets of section 2 always pass through, configs/drugs_acting_as_graph_compounds.csv and configs/non_protein_drug_targets.csv (sections 4 and 5), and, when the builder is given them, the tables of decisions 9.117 and 9.121 (sections 2 and 5).

## 2. Identifier bridge (data/raw/onsides/v3.1.1/ingredient_identifier_bridge.json)

One record per ingredient. Routes to a ChEMBL parent, in precedence order; the first that yields a parent is recorded as bridge_method:

| bridge_method | route | ingredients |
|---|---|---|
| unii_unichem | RxCUI -> UNII (RxNav property UNII_CODE) -> ChEMBL ids (UniChem, FDA SRS source 14) -> parent (molecule_hierarchy); an OMOP id never takes this route | 1,172 |
| name_pref_name | ingredient name equals a ChEMBL pref_name ignoring case -> parent | 448 |
| none | no parent | 246 |

When a route returns several parents the lowest-numbered id is taken and the rest are kept in chembl_parent_alternatives (1 ingredient). 1,620 ingredients have a parent (1,609 distinct), 1,608 a UNII, 53 are OMOP extension ids.

ATC classes (atc_codes_rxnav) come from the RxClass byRxcui response. Entries whose minConcept term type is IN (the ingredient) or PIN (a precise ingredient: a salt or ester such as olmesartan medoxomil under olmesartan, where RxClass attaches many drugs' classes) are kept; MIN entries (multi-ingredient concepts with their own codes) are dropped; an untyped entry is kept only when its RxCUI is the queried one. atc_codes_sider adds drug_atc.tsv codes of the unified SIDER drug; atc_codes_curated, empty in the bridge file, is set by the builder from configs/atc_code_supplements.csv (`--atc-code-supplements`, decision 9.117: a code a label source holds for the substance under another identifier, such as lithium's N05AN01 under SIDER's other lithium identifier), matched by perturbation_id after the identity overrides below, so a merged salt takes the codes of the drug it joins, and nervous_system_atc is recomputed with it; atc_codes is the sorted union of the three. Build of 5 October 2026, before the supplements existed: 1,523 ingredients have a code, 252 an ATC N code.

Unification with SIDER (unification_method), in precedence order:

| unification_method | rule | ingredients |
|---|---|---|
| chembl_parent | the ingredient's parent is among the ChEMBL parents of a SIDER STITCH flat identifier (pubchem_to_chembl_with_parents.json resolved through molecule_parents.json and the mechanism table) | 842 |
| sider_drug_name | otherwise, the ingredient name equals a SIDER drug name ignoring case (drug_names.tsv); this reaches SIDER drugs whose own ChEMBL mapping is empty or resolves to a stereo or racemate variant of the ingredient's parent | 158 |
| none | no SIDER partner; perturbation_id is RXCUI:<id> or OMOP:<id> | 866 |
| identity_override_split | set by the builder (`--perturbation-identity-overrides configs/perturbation_identity_overrides.csv`, decision 9.117; the enantiomers ruled under 9.121 C5): an enantiomer the bridge filed under the SIDER drug of its racemate or other enantiomer, where the forms act differently or are not known to act alike, becomes an OnSIDES-only perturbation (RXCUI:<id>) with its own parent's targets; the SIDER CID it leaves is kept as split_from_sider_pubchem_cid | not applied |
| identity_override_merge | set by the builder from the same table: an ingredient the bridge left a drug of its own (a salt of the same active ion, or the same drug under another name; the row's reason says which) is filed under the SIDER perturbation the row names, taking that drug's name and SIDER ATC codes from an ingredient already unified with it, or else its name from drug_names.tsv (as corrected) and no SIDER ATC codes | not applied |

A row of the third kind, parent (decision 9.121), keeps the ingredient's perturbation and unification_method and replaces the ChEMBL parent the bridge chose, a record without a mechanism row or none at all, by the drug's own record, which holds the mechanism; a merge may name a parent too. The parent the bridge chose is kept as chembl_parent_overridden_from. The overrides are applied when the builder loads the bridge and are not written into the bridge file; an override naming an ingredient the bridge lacks is an error. The counts column is the build of 5 October 2026, before the table existed.

A unified ingredient's perturbation_id is the STITCH flat identifier of the lowest matching PubChem CID (CID1 plus the CID zero-padded to eight digits), the string SIDER reports carry, so the evidence table sees one perturbation with two label slices; the other matching CIDs are sider_collision_pubchem_cids (24 ingredients, 23 parents listed under sider_collisions). The join is parent-to-flat-CID, not parent-to-parent: a STITCH flat identifier drops stereochemistry, so one SIDER CID can carry several ChEMBL parents. sider_cid_chembl_parents lists every parent of the chosen CID and sider_cid_merges_distinct_parents is true when there is more than one (196 unified ingredients; in the qualifying rows 232 reports on 20 perturbations whose merged parents are enantiomer or racemate records of one drug, such as citalopram and escitalopram, bupivacaine and levobupivacaine, modafinil and armodafinil, eszopiclone and zopiclone or eslicarbazepine and licarbazepine; 4 of those perturbations carry two ingredients each, all in the build of 5 October 2026; given the override table, the builder splits escitalopram, eszopiclone and dextroamphetamine off by identity_override_split). A merge of distinct drugs under one CID (betamethasone and dexamethasone, doxorubicin and epirubicin, ephedrine and pseudoephedrine) is therefore visible in the record and in the count unified_cid_merges_distinct_parents, and a later rule change that admits such a pair is caught there.

Mechanism targets: mechanisms.json is keyed by molecule_chembl_id and by parent_molecule_chembl_id (mechanisms_by_parent_and_molecule), the one keying the bridge fetch, the missing-target fetch and the report builder share, so a mechanism ChEMBL records on a salt form counts for the parent. mechanism_target_ids lists the human targets of the parent (organism Homo sapiens or unknown) that mechanism_targets (perturbation/map_drug_targets_to_graph_nodes.py) keeps: a ruled removal of configs/drug_target_exclusions.csv is applied first, a ruled addition of configs/drug_target_additions.csv is appended, and a target whose rows give the parent more than one action type takes the action a row of configs/chembl_action_conflict_resolutions.csv names or, without one, is withheld and not listed (the ruling of 20 November 2026; every run before the three tables, added on 20 November 2026, kept the action of the last row read and every target ChEMBL gives, and no other). single_mechanism_target applies has_dominant_target with one target, a withheld target counting against the cap (passes_single_target_rule with withheld_targets), and nervous_system_atc applies is_nervous_system_atc to atc_codes. The targets of every mechanism under a bridged parent are fetched into targets.json when missing (64 records added on 5 October 2026, which brought the file to 631); a target id still missing after the fetch is listed under errors and is reported by the builder as target_record_missing rather than as a missing graph gene.

Bridge counts of the released file (data/releases/v0.4/onsides/ingredient_identifier_bridge.json, as of the build of 5 October 2026): ingredients 1,866; with_any_mechanism_target 1,134; single_mechanism_target 892; single_target_and_atc_n 150; single_target_and_atc_n_onsides_only 28; unified_with_sider 1,000 over 973 distinct SIDER drugs. The name-bridge comparison (sider_name_bridge_comparison) records that the 861 ingredients whose name equals a SIDER drug name are all unified, 857 to a CID of that name and 4 to another CID through the parent route; 139 ingredients are unified without a name match.

## 3. Statements (aggregate_label_statements)

Term matching: a PT row of vocab_meddra_adverse_effect matches a target symptom when its name is a crosswalk preferred term (the builder's `--crosswalk`: docs/symptom_crosswalk.csv by default and in the build of 5 October 2026, docs/symptom_crosswalk_fine.csv in the v5 step of scripts/rebuild_data_layer.sh). An LLT row matches through the PT it falls under, using the LLT-to-PT pairing SIDER lists per label term in meddra_all_se.tsv.gz (sider_lower_level_to_preferred_terms; 5,805 LLT names paired without redistributing MedDRA); without the pairing an LLT matches only on an exact name, which never happens in the v3.1.1 vocabulary. 21 OnSIDES LLT ids fall under crosswalk PTs this way; a MedDRA LLT-to-PT table kept outside the repository would be the complete solution. The statement keeps the source term type in meddra_term_type.

One statement per (ingredient_id, target_symptom, meddra_id, label_section, source_country) over the events of every label whose RxNorm products contain the ingredient: label_count distinct labels, single_ingredient_label_count of them with exactly one ingredient, labels_of_ingredient_in_country, max_pred1, mean_pred1 and match_methods (the distinct match_method values joined by ";"). Build of 5 October 2026: 5,959 statements, 5,649 PT-coded and 310 LLT-coded.

Label sections exist for US labels only: AR (Adverse Reactions), BW (Boxed Warning) and WP (Warnings and Precautions). Every UK, EU and JP event carries label_section NA, OnSIDES' value for the undivided non-US label whose extraction covers the undesirable-effects section; no US event does (crosstab of the statements: EU 1,550, JP 671 and UK 1,288 NA rows, US 2,216 AR, 20 BW and 214 WP rows).

## 4. Report rows (data/processed/onsides/onsides_reports.parquet)

The 35 shared columns of docs/evidence_reports_spec.md come first (REPORT_COLUMNS), then six OnSIDES rubric columns, then ONSIDES_EXTRA_COLUMNS. Every statement produces a row; qualifies marks the rows that enter the evidence table through assemble_evidence_table.py --extra-reports.

| column | value |
|---|---|
| report_id | `OnSIDES-label|{perturbation_id}|{ingredient_id}|{meddra_id}|{symptom}|{label_section}|{source_country}` |
| perturbation_id, perturbation_type, perturbation_label | the bridge's perturbation_id; "drug"; the SIDER drug name when unified (since 24 October 2026 passed through sider_drug_names.readable_drug_name, which applies configs/sider_drug_name_corrections.csv), else the ingredient name |

| model_description | `human; drug label (OnSIDES v3.1.1, {country}); {target_source} targets {target_chembl_id}:{action_type};...`, where target_source is ChEMBL, or curated for targets taken from the curated tables of section 5 (every row of the build of 5 October 2026 reads ChEMBL); the targets are the ingredient's before any complex narrowing |

## 5. Qualification rule (qualify_ingredient and onsides_reports)

The rule SIDER rows pass in assemble_evidence_table.py, applied per ingredient in the order of the table, then two row-level tests; the first failing test names disqualified_reason. Rows marked "not tested" name a test added after the build of 5 October 2026:

| disqualified_reason | test | statements (build of 5 October 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| excluded_by_curation | the ingredient's perturbation_id is listed in configs/excluded_drug_perturbations.csv (`--excluded-drug-perturbations`, decision 9.121 C4: a drug admitted on its ATC code that acts outside the brain), tested before any other | not tested (added 8 November 2026) |
| no_chembl_parent | the bridge has no ChEMBL parent and no curated target stands in for one (below) | 479 |
| chembl_action_conflict_unresolved | the parent's mechanism targets are all withheld for an unresolved ChEMBL action conflict (section 2), so none is left | not tested (added 20 November 2026) |
| no_single_mechanism_target | the parent's human mechanism targets are not exactly one (has_dominant_target, max_targets 1), a withheld target counting as one. Since 8 October 2026 `--max-drug-targets` sets the cap and 0 lifts it (pass the assembler the same value); an ingredient ChEMBL gives no target can take one from the tables below | 2,125 |
| no_atc_code | the ingredient has no ATC code at all; there is no waiver, since a SIDER drug without a code does not qualify either | 130 |
| atc_not_nervous_system | no ATC N code (has_admitted_atc_code with the group N prefixes, the version 1 proxy for central action). Since 10 October 2026 `--admitted-atc-prefixes <csv>` replaces group N by a written list of groups and substances (atc_prefix column; pass the assembler the same file), and the reason is then atc_not_admitted | 1,985 |
| combination_label_only | every label behind the statement is a combination product (single_ingredient_label_count 0): the label of carbidopa/levodopa says nothing about carbidopa alone | 96 |
| target_record_missing | the target has no record in targets.json, so no gene symbol can be mapped | 0 |
| no_graph_node | the target's gene symbols are not graph nodes | 0 |

Graph compounds and curated targets. An ingredient with no mechanism target whose parent is in configs/drugs_acting_as_graph_compounds.csv takes that graph compound as its one target, as a SIDER drug does; so, since 20 November 2026, does an ingredient with mechanism targets whose parent's row there sets moves_mechanism_to_compound_edges (melatonin; read_compounds_moving_their_mechanism), since its ChEMBL targets move onto the compound's own edges when the drug-entry build merges the drug onto the compound. An ingredient still without a target takes, by its perturbation_id, the role "mechanism" rows of the curated tables the builder is given (`--curated-drug-targets configs/psychiatric_drug_additions_v4_draft.csv configs/curated_drug_targets.csv`, decisions 9.117 and 9.121 C3), even where the bridge found no ChEMBL parent, and its rows then read curated in model_description. A target withheld for an action conflict keeps the ingredient from both fallbacks, which are for an ingredient ChEMBL gives no target. The builder's flags for the curated tables of sections 2 and 5 (`--perturbation-identity-overrides`, `--atc-code-supplements`, `--curated-drug-targets`, `--excluded-drug-perturbations`) default to none; the v5 step of scripts/rebuild_data_layer.sh passes all four, with `--admitted-atc-prefixes configs/admitted_atc_prefixes.csv`, and the assembler the same files. `--chembl-action-conflict-resolutions` defaults to configs/chembl_action_conflict_resolutions.csv, and beside onsides_reports.parquet the builder writes chembl_action_conflicts.csv, one row for each drug and target whose ChEMBL rows disagree on the action, resolved or withheld, and counts both in onsides_summary.json.

Qualifying in the build of 5 October 2026: 1,144 reports from 127 ingredients and 123 perturbations (102 shared with SIDER, 21 OnSIDES-only); by unification 100 ingredients through the parent route, 6 by drug name and 21 without a SIDER partner.

## 6. Limitations text

Clauses joined with "; ": "label statement without frequency"; "{label_count} label(s) in {country}"; the section ("adverse reactions section", "boxed warning", "warnings and precautions section", "non-US label, section not recorded by OnSIDES" or "unsectioned mention" for any other value); "combination-product labels only" when single_ingredient_label_count is 0; "string match without model score" or "extraction score {score} of 1"; "no ATC code known, central action not tested" when the ingredient has no ATC code; "dated by the OnSIDES v3.1.1 release (2026-04-22), an upper bound".

## 7. Pair-level overlap with SIDER (build_onsides_reports.py)

Two SIDER sides are measured for the qualifying OnSIDES (perturbation_id, symptom) pairs. The qualified side is the SIDER-label pairs of the assembled table (data/processed/evidence_full/evidence_reports.parquet, source SIDER-label, relation induces): the 543 pairs of SIDER drugs that passed SIDER's own ChEMBL mapping, single-target and ATC N rules, which the time split trains on. The raw side is every PT adverse-event row of SIDER 4.1 whose term is in the crosswalk (5,248 (identifier, symptom) pairs), looked up for every STITCH flat identifier the bridge links to the perturbation (the unified CID and its collisions, and for an ingredient an identity override split off, the CID whose stereo-flattened 2015 labels pooled it), independent of SIDER's qualification.

Build of 5 October 2026: 587 OnSIDES pairs; 283 in both; 260 SIDER only; 304 absent from the qualified table, of which 204 are on 2015 SIDER labels and 100 are later-slice candidates (41 on perturbations SIDER also holds, 59 on OnSIDES-only perturbations). The design's time split (section 6.1) trains on the SIDER 2015 pairs and tests on the later-slice candidates, not on every pair absent from the qualified table; a candidate may still have been on a 2015 label that SIDER's extraction missed, and OnSIDES rows are dated by the release.

## 8. Known limits

The stereo-flattened SIDER identifier can pool stereoisomers of one drug under one perturbation (section 2). The ATC N proxy still decides central action in this builder (has_admitted_atc_code; `--admitted-atc-prefixes` can replace group N by a written list of ATC prefixes, section 5); the measured Kp,uu,brain admission rule of 12 October 2026 (experiments/apply_central_action_rule.py, which writes configs/centrally_acting_drugs.csv) is not read by it, so no measured blood-brain barrier flag has replaced the proxy here. The LLT pairing covers only the terms SIDER listed. The full table was re-assembled with the 5 October 2026 onsides_reports.parquet in the same commit (d00ddc5) and released as data/releases/v0.4/evidence_full (3,910 observations; 7,488 reports, 1,144 of them OnSIDES-label).
