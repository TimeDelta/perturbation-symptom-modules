# Grade-A monogenic pairs with a negative report (soft_priors.md section 7, "the 34")

Built on 2026-10-21 from repository main at 2f21e2b. The repo's own assembler, `assemble_evidence_table.assemble`, was run on the monogenic part only (no SIDER or ChEMBL), with these inputs:

- HPO release v2026-09-01 (phenotype.hpoa `#version: 2026-09-02`), Orphadata products 6 and 1 (JDBOR 2026-06-23; product 1 sha256 matches the pin), and `docs/symptom_crosswalk.csv` (24 symptoms)
- `--seed-gain-of-function-genes-positive`, as for v4
- graph gene set from `data/releases/v0.4/graph_full`

**Result: 38 monogenic records with `negative_report_count > 0`: 34 grade A and 4 grade C. The count is 34.** Per symptom (cognitive_impairment 27, irritability 2, psychosis 2, parkinsonism 3, anxiety, fatigue, depressed_mood and self_injury 1 each) and all-negative per symptom, these match `docs/physiology_audit.md` section 5 exactly.

Of the 34:
- **13 are all-negative** (`positive_report_count == 0`). They carry grade A (weight 0.25) only because `grade_weighting_rows` keeps 0-of-N rows. The loader drops them (`positive_report_count > 0`), so they are not labels, contrary to "which makes them labels" in section 7.
- **21 are conflicting** (positive and negative reports). All 21 are trained positives in the loader. Under the registered better_v2 selection, 13 are kept and 8 are set aside (frequency below 0.30 or missing).
- Across all 38 records, 15 are all-negative (13 of them in the label grades A or B).

Classes over the 21 conflicting pairs:
- 1 (true within-entry): 2 (GLRX5, RRP12)
- 1b (within-entry, different terms, granularity): 2 (LAMB1, RAB3A)
- 2a: 2, both mixed (DNM1L: repo typing vs the PMID disagree; WFS1: dominant vs recessive plus a same-disease disagreement)
- 2b: 13, including PDE10A (same disease by exact mapping, 1b-like) and TYMP (same disease, frequencies compatible)
- 2c: 1 (SCARB2)
- artefact: 1 (POLG: the negative comes from a TYMP cohort)

Crosswalk-caused (the negative is on a narrower HPO term than the positive, and the descendant closure turns it into absence of the whole construct): yes for 8, partly for 5.

Columns:
- Frequencies are the raw phenotype.hpoa values; HPO qualifiers are spelled out.
- "crosswalk causes conflict?": yes means the negation sits on a descendant term (for example Dementia), while the positive is on the root or a sibling branch of the construct.
- Inheritance comes from the phenotype.hpoa aspect I rows of OMIM entries; Orphanet entries carry none.
- "Repo typing" is the result of `allele_mechanisms.disease_mechanisms`, then `first_source_unless_absent` with Gene2Phenotype (all-panel download of 2026-10-21), then `apply_adjudications`. None of these genes is in `configs/allele_mechanism_adjudications.csv`, `configs/neomorphic_alleles.csv` or `docs/allele_perturbations.md`.
- PMIDs were read from PubMed metadata (titles and abstracts).
